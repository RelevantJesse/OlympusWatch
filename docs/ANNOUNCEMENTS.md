## Forever community / Reddit

**Title:** OlympusWatch: recent nearby PvP sightings and quick help requests for Forever

I made OlympusWatch for those spontaneous world PvP encounters where someone spots an opponent and wants nearby help.

It discovers guild names containing Olympus and shares reports of their visible, PvP-flagged opponents with other addon users. Open `/ow` to see your current outdoor zone's sightings from the last 15 minutes, select an encounter, then map it, whisper the reporter, request help or ask for a party invite.

It works for Alliance and Horde, keeps PvP/PvE/RP/Hardcore lists separate, and groups crowded reports into encounter areas so the UI doesn't become a wall of player names. You can hide false matches personally, and those choices survive sync. Players don't need a companion app.

Locations are approximate observer positions, reports age out, and layers are unconfirmed. Cities and instances are excluded from encounter reporting. This isn't a live census or guaranteed enemy location.

Download 0.8.0: https://github.com/RelevantJesse/OlympusWatch/releases/tag/v0.8.0

Source, guide and feedback: https://github.com/RelevantJesse/OlympusWatch

I'd appreciate feedback on the nearby encounter workflow, reporting reliability and readability in crowded areas.

## Addon development / UI community

**Title:** [Addon] OlympusWatch for Forever: one-window nearby PvP reports and coordination

I released OlympusWatch 0.8.0 for Forever (Interface 16001). Its focus is quick nearby encounters: grouped reports from the last 15 minutes, a selected-report detail panel, and map/help/whisper/invite actions.

The interface has Nearby, Guilds and Settings tabs. Guild faction, visibility and source have separate columns; personal hide/show choices persist independently of signed global changes. Crowd reporting is bounded, and PvP/PvE/RP/Hardcore data is separated.

Sighting reports cover visible, PvP-flagged opposing-faction members of watched Olympus guilds. Observer locations are approximate, layers are unconfirmed, and reports are shared through reachable in-game peers. Cities and instances are excluded from encounter reporting.

Download and source: https://github.com/RelevantJesse/OlympusWatch

Feedback and reproducible bugs are welcome through GitHub Issues. Offline behavioral, authority, minimap, dashboard and package checks passed; live delivery depends on the game's channel routing.
